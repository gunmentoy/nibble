# Nibble - Mood-Based Restaurant Discovery Platform

## 🎯 Overview

**Nibble** is a modern web application that eliminates restaurant decision paralysis by helping users discover the perfect dining destination in under 60 seconds. Instead of endless scrolling through reviews, users select a mood (Romantic, Cozy, Adventurous, etc.) and get curated restaurant collections matched to their vibe.

**Tagline:** *"Discover restaurants by mood, not menu."*

---

## 💡 The Problem We Solve

- **23 minutes** average time spent choosing a restaurant
- **67%** of people feel overwhelmed by too many dining options
- **73%** research restaurants online but still struggle with discovery
- Existing platforms (Yelp, Google Maps, Instagram) are either overwhelming or require multiple steps

---

## 🚀 Project Scope

### Target Market
- **College/University Students** (18-24): 180,000+ in Metro Vancouver
- **Young Professionals** (25-35): ~400,000 individuals
- Dining frequency: 2-3 times/week, $25-45/meal budget
- Tech-savvy: 85% use mobile devices for restaurant discovery

### Launch Objectives (Year 1)
- ✅ MVP Launch: April 26, 2025
- 🎯 500 active monthly users by Month 6
- 🎯 25+ local Vancouver restaurant partners
- 💰 $500-800 monthly recurring revenue by Month 12
- 📍 Break-even: Month 9

---

## 💰 Business Model

### For Users
- **Free, ad-supported** mood-based discovery platform
- No subscription required

### For Restaurants (Tiered Advertising)
- **Basic Listing**: Free
- **Featured Placement**: $75/month (Priority in mood collections, enhanced listing, analytics)
- **Sponsored Swipes**: $125/month (Sponsored badge, higher visibility, top of listings)
- **Banner Ads**: $200/month (Premium placement, all features)

## 🌐 Pages & Features

### 1. **Homepage** (`index.html`)
- Hero section with tagline and CTA
- 8 mood selection cards (Romantic, Cozy, Adventurous, Quick Bite, Celebrate, Casual, Trendy, Scenic)
- Featured restaurants showcase
- Why Nibble benefits section
- Call-to-action for restaurant partners

### 2. **Browse Results** (`browse.html`)
- Mood-based filtering (currently showing "Romantic Restaurants")
- Filter options: Cuisine, Price, Distance, Rating
- Sponsored restaurant listings with priority placement
- Regular restaurant result cards
- Favorite/save functionality
- Load more results button

### 3. **Restaurant Profile** (`restaurant-profile.html`)
- Image gallery with thumbnails
- Full restaurant details (name, rating, cuisine, hours, contact)
- Quick action buttons (Directions, Website, Book Table)
- About section with description
- Highlights (features, amenities)
- Menu preview
- Embedded map location
- Similar restaurant recommendations

### 4. **User Account** (`account.html`)
- Dashboard with stats (saved restaurants, visited places, reviews)
- Favorites management (grid view with remove option)
- Preferences (location, dietary restrictions, price range)
- Security settings (password, connected devices)
- Notification preferences

### 5. **Partner Dashboard** (`partner-dashboard.html`)
- Performance metrics (views, clicks, favorites, CTR)
- Current subscription plan display
- Analytics over time (chart placeholder)
- Traffic breakdown by mood
- Profile editing
- Billing & subscription management
- Detailed analytics
- Settings

### 6. **Partner Landing Page** (`partner-landing.html`)
- Benefits overview (6 key advantages)
- How it works (4-step process)
- Transparent pricing table
- Call-to-action for sign-up
